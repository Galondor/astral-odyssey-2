# v0.3.2.0

## Features

- Solo and PvP combat now use one Action and one Quick Action in either order.
- Potions and some skills are Quick Actions; your Action can instead be used as a second Quick Action.
- Your remaining Action (⚔) and Quick Action (⚡) are shown as icons.
- Some enemy's now have telegraphed heavy attacks.
- Interactive combat tutorial.
- Gear dropped by non-boss enemies now has an item level no higher than the enemy's level.
- Ultimate Skills, 2 unlocked at level 25, and only 1 can be equipped at a time.
- Passive Skills: 15 passives that unlock between levels 15 and 70, and up to 3 can be equipped.
- Regions, solo dungeons, and the Labyrinth now support multi-enemy encounters.
- Basic & Elite enemies can't drop gear above their level.
- Skill Gemstones, which grant +1 level to a specific skill and can overcap the skill's normal level cap.
- Skill Gemstones have a 1% chance to drop from most bosses.

### Material Crafting & Professions
- You can now craft items in the Forge.
- Three crafting professions, Weaponry, Armor, and Jewelry, each with their own progression through level 50.
- Every material has hidden properties you can discover by crafting with them.
- A crafting book inside the Forge will retain all learned properties of materials you discover.
- Mythic items, only obtainable by crafting them.
  - Every stat is T7, they are always Holofoil, and they gain a 7th wildcard substat that can be any substat, even a duplicate.
- Blacksmith's Hammers and Precision Tools are craft-only gear for the new Crafting & Gathering equipment slots found in your character.
  - These items will enhance your crafting experiene.      
- 57 new crafting materials:
  - Ore, hide, cloth, wood, precious metal, and gems for every region.
  - Hammer and Precision Tools parts.
  - Crafting catalysts.
