# v0.2.3.0

## Interface

- Made the mobile sidebar scroll horizontally below the top navigation again.
- Kept inventory controls in place while scrolling through item lists.
- Equipped items in the gemstone tab are highlighted.

## Features

- Added sorting and filtering options to the inventory.
- Added a bug reporting tool.
- Materials and Gemstones can now be bought and sold in the marketplace.
- Added rune system, consumables used to modify items.
- Added Item Certificates, which can rename owned items, drops from elites and bosses.
- Added Architect's Rune, modify an items main stat. Drops from elites and bosses from the dungeon.
- Added an English inappropriate-name filter for item names and character names.

## Gameplay

- Gear Trader stock improves at each town reputation rank.
- Tier's on main stats are now calculated by the total value of the stat, rather than the base roll.
- Rebalanced all enemy stats.
- Reduced Burn heal reduction from 50% -> 10%.
- Arcane Battery can now be upgraded 10 times from 3.
- Intelligence now grants 2 magic damage per point and no longer increases max power; characters gain 1 max power per level.
- Added Power as an offensive and defensive item substat.
- Added Unique items obtainable from the dungeon, Unique's guarntee a minimum of T4 on their stats and have a powerful special ability.
- Added new quests for dungeon content, quests now give better item bases depending on the tier of the quest.

## Dungeons!

- The minimum level to enter a dungeon is 12.
- Dungeons randomly generate their layout with a mix of enemy encounters and puzzles.
- There are 6 types of dungeons with their own unique enemies and rewards.
- There are 12 unique dungeon bosses with their very own Unique item drop, these are extremely rare!
- Exlixir's of Name Changing have a 5% chance to drop from any dungeon boss.
- Architect's Runes drop from dungeon elites at 5% and dungeon bosses at 10%.
