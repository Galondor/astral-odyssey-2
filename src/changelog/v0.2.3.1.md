# v0.2.3.1

## Interface

- Cleaned up the sidebar on desktop.
- Added Arena tab.

## Features

- You can now favorite items by right clicking them. They appear first in your inventory and can't be sold.
- Equipment can now roll as a Holofoil with a randomly generated 2 color gradient background.

## Gameplay

- Significantly reduced the amount of material drops from the dungeon.
- Tuned enemy stats for better balance.
- Backstab power cost increased from 2 -> 3.
- Fireball power cost increased from 4 -> 5.
- Frost Lance power cost increased from 3 -> 4.
- Frost Lance stun chance reduced per level by 2.5%.
- Soul Siphon healing reduced from 10% -> 5% per level.
- Soul Siphon damage scaling reduced per level by 5%.
- Burning healing penalty increased from 10% -> 25%.
- Agility evasion reduced to 1% per 8 points (from 1% per 5 points).

## Fixes

- Fixed Item Certificates tooltip not working when they're dropped.
- Fixed the leaderboard not displaying correctly on smaller screens.
