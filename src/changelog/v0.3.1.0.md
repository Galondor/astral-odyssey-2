# v0.3.1.0

## Balance Changes
- Resilience buffed from 1:3 Defense to 1:2 Defense
- The World Boss is immune to percent-based damage.
- The Colosseum enemies are now a fixed level based on the wave.
- Solo dungeon bosses have roughly 15% less health and attack and 10% less defense.
- Boss critical hit chances are reduced by 20% across the board.
- The Unwritten King has roughly 15% less health and attack and 12.5% less defense.
- Gelatinous King has roughly 25% lower health, attack, and defense, weaker poison and multi-hit attacks, less healing, and a weaker enrage.
- Group dungeon enemies have roughly 40% more health, 30% more attack, and 10% more defense.

## Features
- Critical Resistance and Status Resistance can roll as substats on helmets, chestplates, gauntlets, leg armor, boots, rings, and necklaces.
- Added the Forge's Upgrading tab, unequipped items can now be upgraded up to 10 times increasing the item level and substats with each upgrade.
- Changed how many base sockets items can have by rarity.
  - Common/Uncommon: 0 base sockets
  - Rare: 1 base socket
  - Epic: 1-2 base sockets randomly rolled
  - Legendary/Unique: 2 base sockets
- Holofoil's now grant a minimum of T5 stats, an extra gemstone socket, and increased item value.
- New substats: Critical Resistance and Status Resistance can now appear on various equipment pieces.
- New Stamina Potion & Cleansing Potions added, buyable from the Apothecary.
- 2 New Runes, Rune of Ascension and Polishing Rune.
- Each rune can now be used once per item.


### Travel & Quests Revamp
- Traveling no longer automatically moves you to a new region randomly.
- Wayfinder's Compass now reduces portal fare by 25%.
- Every region now has a level range and an entry requirement
- Portal fares are now fixed per region instead of scaling with your level.
- All overworld enemies were remastered for their region's level range. Enemies spawn from 1 level below to 2 levels above you, capped to the region's range.
- Quests are now ranked by rarity instead of D through S+, and each rarity sets the type of work:
  - Common: hunt basic enemies or fetch common materials.
  - Uncommon: larger basic hunts or fetches.
  - Rare and Epic: hunt elite enemies or fetch rarer regional, solo dungeon, or Labyrinth materials.
  - Legendary: defeat a region boss or deliver a large haul of materials.
  - Unique: defeat a solo dungeon boss or clear a group dungeon and defeat its boss.
- Quests only come from regions you can enter that suit your level, and they are scaled to your level.
- Reputation no longer locks quests. Reputation is still earned from quests.
- Gathering quests now show how many of the requested material you hold.
- Every potion can now be a quest reward.
- Epic, Legendary, and Unique quests have a very small chance to include a rune reward.

## Quality of Life
- The Apothecary now displays potion cards with details and a quantity-based purchase popup.
- Consumable cards now open a details popup with Use and Favorite actions instead of using them immediately.
- Consumable and items can now be deleted.
- Item details now display the number of upgrades below the subtitle.
- The Hunter's Horn Key Item can now be toggled on and off.
- Gemstones are now socketed and unsocketed directly from the item popup menu.
- Item cards now visually display socketed gemstones.
- Removed socket text tags from item popups.

## Interface
- Improved layouts for laptops.

## Bug Fixes
- Gear trader stock now properly generates items based on the player's current reputation. (No more only commons)
