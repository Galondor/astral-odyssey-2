# v0.3.0.0

## Interface
- New UI and colorways throughout the entire game.
- Mobile UI massively improved for usability.
- Cleaned up the top bar, now has a search bar to find other players and a quick access menu for profile and settings.
- Items are now clicked on to open a detailed view with all relevent options.
- Item tooltip only shows the items stats.
- Gemstones inventory is easier to navigate.


## Features
- Players can now have up to 3 different characters. (Cross character storage coming soon)
  - Characters can be deleted.
- Added a simple tutorial to guide new players. (A more comprehensive tutorial will come after more content is added.)
- New players receive starting gear based on their chosen stats and class to help with progression.
- Players have public facing profiles that anyone can see, displaying their characters stats and gear.
  - You can opt out of having your profile publicly visible in the new settings in the quick access menu.
- Control buttons can be selected based on their numerical value, instead of only clicked on.
- Added tooltips to all the locations in town giving brief description of them.
- Added a new social tab where you can become friends with other players and view invites.
- Titles can be earned and shown off on your profile.
- Holofoils have 8 different effects that are randomly rolled when obtained.
- Rune of Unpredictability, used to randomly alter an item. Sometimes it may improve the item, other times worsen it.
- Prismatic Alloy, used to make an item Holofoil or reroll the effect of an existing Holofoil.
- Mass gear and gem selling in the gear trader.
- 4 New regions to travel through with new challenges and enemies to face.
- New reputation ranks in town. (More quests coming soon)
- You can 'bet half' in blackjack now.
- The scarecrow is real...

### Adventures
- Renamed the Dungeons tab to Adventures.
- Adventures include:
  - Solo dungeons at Level 12.
  - Labyrinth at level 22.
  - World Boss at level 35.
  - Group Dungeons starting at level 40.

### Labyrinth
- The Labyrinth is a challenging weekly maze you travel through, facing traps, puzzles, and enemies. At the end stands a powerful guardian that must be slain to claim your rewards, and if you're lucky you'll obtain their unique drop.
- The Labyrinth resets weekly, and you can only clear it once per week.
- You will find Relics and Relic Fragments in the labyrinth.
- You will use Relics at the new Forge in town to craft custom gear using Relic Fragments to pick and choose the stats you want.

### World Boss
- A tremendous foe that requires many players to defeat, each player may face the boss 3 times per cycle.
- If the boss is defeated, all players with at least 1% damage contribution receive rewards based on their performance.
- The top damage dealers receive special rewards and have a much higher chance of getting the boss's unique drop.
- The World Boss resets every 3 days.

### Group Dungeons (Experimental)
- Group Dungeons are intense dungeons designed for 3 players to face together.
- There are 3 dungeons, a level 40, 60, and 80 dungeon with their own unique twists and challenges.
- Players create lobbies that other players can join publicly or by invitation only.
- There are 4 stages in a dungeon, with a checkpoint inbetween each stage.
- Dungeons have a 15 minute timer and a wipe loses 2 minutes in addition to respawning at the last checkpoint.
- There's a chat feature for communicating with your party during a run.

### Support skills
- To help with the challenge of Group Dungeons, new support based skills have been added.
  - **Mending Light** heals an ally.
  - **Renewal** gives an ally health regeneration.
  - **Battle Hymn** buffs the whole party.
  - **Aegis** shields an ally with a barrier.
  - **Purify** cleanses an ally's debuffs.
  - **Provoke** draws enemy attention to you and braces you for the blows.

## Arena
- Test your mettle against other players and ferocious gladiators in the Arena.
- Arena modes are bare-bones for now, more features coming soon.

### Colosseum
- An endless wave of increasingly difficult enemies to test your true combat prowess.
- Can only attempt a run every 15 minutes.
- A leaderboard tracks the highest wave cleared by each player.

### Contest (Experimental)
- Face off against another player in a live 1v1 duel, highest speed acts first.
- Victories grant a Proof of Victory material.
- Leaderboard coming soon.

## Balance Changes
- Players get 1 stamina every other level instead of every level.
- Players get 1 skill point every 3 levels instead of every other level.
- Players get 1 max power every 2 levels instead of every level.
- Intelligence is 1 Magic Attack instead of 2.
- Min & Max magic attack roles higher than min & max attack can on items.
- Vitality now grants 5 Health for every 2 points.
- Crit Chance and Evasion are capped at 60%.
- All stats on items roll significantly lower than before.
- Unqiue items now have 6 substats instead of 5.
- All enemies across the board's stats have been adjusted to be inline with the new player progression.
