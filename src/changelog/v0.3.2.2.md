# v0.3.2.2

## Features
- You can now extract a dropped Unique's blueprint, which will allow you to craft that unique in the forge.

### Classes
- Classes require less strict stat allocations now, instead they're percentage based on your entire core stat pool.
- Single stat classes require 60% of the corresponding core stat.
- Dual classes require 40% of each corresponding core stat.
- Specialist requires 15% of each stat, with no more than 50% in a single stat.
- Swordsman and Spellblade now have distinct combat loops and dedicated signature actions. Swordsmen build Resolve by defending and blocking, then choose when to retaliate. Spellblades weave melee and magic attacks into Fusion Strike.
- All seven classes have branching mastery trees, shared-skill transformations, and unlockable class techniques. Techniques use regular skill slots; signatures do not.
- Mastery starts with 1 point, gaining another every 5 levels. Each class remembers its own allocation of the same earned budget; only the current tree is active. Mastery resets are free outside combat and do not spend or refund shared skill points.
- Class changes preserve and restore skill loadouts without changing equipment. Class identity and mastery are locked for each encounter; meters do not carry into new encounters.
- Stat allocations, equipment changes, and upgrades that change your class now preview the new class and loadout before any costs, items, or rewards are committed.
- Tacticians build Advantage by surveying with Defend/Guard or exploiting defending and debuffed enemies, then spend it on a defense-piercing Precision Strike.
- Spellcasters build Instability to amplify magic at increasing spell costs, then vent it with Arcane Release or the defensive Stabilize technique. Basic magic attacks remain free.
- Duelists can deliberately prepare Riposte Stance, earning Tempo from a successful parry for their next melee action. An unused stance expires before their next turn; committed group telegraphs cannot be parried.
- Tricksters prepare Tricks through damaging actions and choose between a stronger-attack Cash Out finisher and reliable control through Sabotage.
- Specialists rotate offense, protection, and support to build Adaptation, spending it on a stronger-attack Improvise that also heals. Field Mending adds a targeted recovery option, and Battle Hymn can shield allies.
- Existing class bonuses remain alongside the new loops. All class signatures, mastery effects, resource meters, and saved builds work in solo, Contest, and group combat.

## Quality of Life
- Reagents now display the owned quantity in the forge reagent selection.
- Reagents now show a detailed popup when clicked during crafting.
- The player inventory should be less laggy now.
