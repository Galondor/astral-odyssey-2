# v0.3.2.2

## Features
- You can now extract a dropped Unique's blueprint, which will allow you to craft that unique in the forge.

### Classes
- Classes require less strict stat allocations now, instead they're percentage based on your entire core stat pool.
- Single stat classes require 60% of the corresponding core stat.
- Dual classes require 40% of each corresponding core stat.
- Specialist requires 15% of each stat, with no more than 50% in a single stat.
- All classes now have branching mastery trees, skill buffs, and unlockable class techniques that consume a normal skill slot.

## Quality of Life
- Made crafting UI more user-friendly. 
- Crafting now keeps the Craft button near the top of the preview, with detailed rarity chances available on demand.
- Reagents now display the owned quantity in the forge reagent selection.
- Reagents now show a detailed popup when clicked during crafting.
- The player inventory should be less laggy now.

## Balance
- Gear Power rolls are now fixed by tier instead of item level: T0 and T1 grant 1, then T2 through T9 grant 2 through 9.
- Enemies above level 30 get progressively stronger.
- Colosseum enemies are 50% stronger.
